# 30-second demo video: script

Screen recording of the app at 1920 × 1080. Voice-over at about 75 words, calm. Captions on, because judges often watch muted. Music is optional and must be licensed.

| Time | Screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | Calendar first lighting up, year by year | "For 25 years, NASA satellites have counted the world's fires." |
| 4–9 s | Two-eyes panel; press **Translate**: the raw line jumps in 2012, then folds down | "But a new, sharper satellite arrived in 2012, so raw counts jump. That jump isn't real." |
| 9–15 s | Draw a shape over central Portugal; fire races round the edge; embers ignite | "CEDRUS puts every satellite on one scale. Draw any area and get its fire calendar since 2000." |
| 15–20 s | Click October 2017: ghost flame, "10× a normal October" | "Is this month normal? Here, ten times normal: the October 2017 fires." |
| 20–25 s | Scroll to **How We Know**: the NOT CONFIRMED stamp, then two CONFIRMED stamps | "We tested it like an exam, on regions it had never seen. The first one failed, and we published that. Then it passed." |
| 25–30 s | Switch to ES, then عربي; end on the "Right now" strip | "In English, Spanish and Arabic, and live from NASA's feed. CEDRUS." |

**Recording tips**
- Use Story mode for the October 2017 moment.
- Start with Effects on.
- Hide the browser bar: full screen, F11.
