Team CEDRUS (Beirut), NASA Space Apps 2026 challenge "Harmonization of MODIS and VIIRS Hot Spots" (event 14-15 Nov 2026; challenge authored by CONAE Argentina). Required: harmonize FIRMS MODIS (C6.1, Terra+Aqua 1 km, 2000-) and VIIRS 375 m (S-NPP 2012-, NOAA-20 2018-, NOAA-21) active-fire hotspots into one consistent series, then a web app: burning-activity calendar (hotspot counts month x year, 2000s-present) for a user-selected area (draw on map or lat/lon), current month vs historical median, anomalies vs multi-year average, accessible color ramps, downloadable CSV of hotspots.

Facts: S-NPP data delivery ends 2 Nov 2026 13:00 UTC. Terra/Aqua drifting (overpass time changes since about 2020-2022; Giglio says effects "abruptly increased in late 2024"), shutting down late 2026 to 2027. FIRMS keyless country archives: https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/data/country/<modis|viirs-snpp|viirs-jpss1>/<year>/<sensor>_<year>_<Country>.csv (through 2025; 2026 and NOAA-21 need API MAP_KEY). Columns MODIS: latitude,longitude,brightness,scan,track,acq_date,acq_time,satellite,instrument,confidence(0-100),version,bright_t31,frp,daynight,type. VIIRS: bright_ti4,scan,track,...,confidence(l/n/h),version,bright_ti5,frp,daynight,type.

Our current state (Portugal, 0.25 deg cells, monthly): ratio r(cell,month) shrunk to national monthly ratio, train 2012-02..2019-12, test 2020-2025 ("pretend MODIS retired"): cell-month WAPE 52.8% vs one-ratio 53.5% vs naive merge 200.9%; season-date error 6.8 vs 10.1 days. VIIRS about 2.7x MODIS pixels. 2024 outlier (+45%). Poisson noise likely about 50% at these counts.

Competitors (about 45 public repos): Kiln Watch (Bangladesh; cloud-free-cell denominator via Earth Engine, Aqua anchor, season-block bootstrap 95% CI, pre-registered, Chow break test), samuelakosa/firecal (208 countries, 0.1 deg fire cell-days, shrinkage to world prior k=2.653, outage detection, LOYO), Ember-Line (VIIRS-equivalent weekly log1p, Huber/Theil-Sen/quantile-map/isotonic selection, cross-conformal intervals, polished Next.js), sagoresarker/nasa (k(month)*MODIS + small-fire floor, hierarchical shrinkage, 12-14% monthly error, detection probability vs FRP analysis), stachulemko/firecal (LightGBM Poisson daily, leakage-checked), Agun-Panjika (NB+LightGBM, burned-area seam check), flare-chronos (H3 fractional footprints, scan-angle FRP operator, effort normalization).
