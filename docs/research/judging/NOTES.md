CRITERIA 2025 (each scored 1-5, at every level of judging)
Impact / Creativity / Validity / Relevance / Presentation. "Influence" not in the 2025 text (only in a Scribd summary of the 2024 guide; unverified).
Validity text explicitly includes completeness + usability. Relevance text: NASA data "core component ... rather than a superficial addition".
Presentation: "Judges evaluate the demo and full project to compute this score." Guide urges equal attention to Presentation.
Submission guide: demo "considered", "but the majority of judges' evaluation will be based on your project submission".

DEMO RULES 2025: video <=30 s (English subtitles) OR deck <=7 slides incl. title; exceeding = ineligible for Global Judging. Public link, no login.
Suggested video timing: 0:00 names, 0:02 challenge + why, 0:05 title + tagline, 0:10 how it works/user experience/NASA data, 0:20-0:30 the future.
Slides must show: team name, member names, challenge; image of project, user experience, how space-agency data made it possible.

FORM FIELDS 2025: Project Name; High-Level Summary; Link to Demo; Link to Final Project; Detailed Description; NASA Data (required for Global Award); Space Agency Partner & Other Data (list ALL code/text/images incl. open source; CONAE is a listed partner); Use of AI; verification checkboxes incl. "Confirm All Work is Original".
AI 2025: allowed; say where/how; AI images/videos need visible watermark; no NASA branding in generated content.
AI 2026 FAQ: renamed "Super Intelligence (SI)"; "Each team's project submission must include a description of how SI tools were used".
License: original content OSI-licensed; default Apache-2.0; licenses of third-party materials must be identified.

RISK: FAQ 2026 "Teams are not allowed to begin working on the challenges prior to the hackathon"; site banner "Do not begin work on your projects until the hackathon begins." + originality checkbox. CEDRUS has pre-built Portugal results. Must resolve with Local Lead / disclose.

2026 dates: guides 13 Nov; event 14-15 Nov; Nominees/Finalists/HM Dec 2026; Winners Jan 2027.

WINNER PATTERNS
- Team I.O. 2024 Local Impact, G.R.O.W.: FIRMS + LLM voice agent; story of one farmer (Gabriel, Ribeirao Preto, 24 Aug, 10 acres, ~USD150k); live Netlify app + GitHub + YouTube demo + Loom; AI use detailed. (They pasted an OpenAI key in the description - do not do this.) Challenge: Community Mapping, not a fire challenge.
- QUENARIS 2025 Local Impact: Arequipa water/queuna; drone + HLS VIs + FIRMS refs; 5,000 seedlings ready, December planting plan, business model; 30-second YouTube demo (lengthSeconds 30); vercel app; long data-reference list; AI tools listed per tool.
- Twisters 2025 Best Use of Technology: SkySense, NASA POWER + Giovanni + GES DISC; live custom domain; Drive video; honest AI statement ("AI was not used to replace our work").
- SpaceGenes+ 2025 Best Use of Science: single NASA dataset (GeneLab OSD-288), a clear explicit formula (synergy > sum of single effects), dashboard; Framer site + Drive demo.
- Resonant Exoplanets 2025 Best Use of Data: end-to-end pipeline, "validate signals with physics models", "reduces false positives", auto PDF reports; YouTube demo; Vercel app.
- GaamaRamma 2024 Best Use of Data: WaterWise irrigation dashboard, OpenET + Worldview; "focus on actions rather than raw numbers"; YouTube demo + Vercel.
- Starflock 2022 Best Use of Data: FIREX-AQ smoke chemistry, 20M rows, normalization + logistic regression; GitHub + Google Slides demo; app only as roadmap.
