# Competitor sweep, 13 Nov 2026

GitHub keyword search plus code and docs reading. Demos were checked for HTTP 200 only, not rendered. Numbers are each team's own claims unless marked as read in their code.

## Ranked threats
1. **squadronzero/project-shift (SHIFT)**, live on Pages
   - Method: a ratio of totals per 2.5° cell and calendar month, fitted on the 2012-19 overlap and shrunk to a 10° parent. The method was read in their docs and equations.
   - Coverage: global, any drawn area, 2003 to 28 Oct 2026.
   - Validation: a backward test with ~4% error on 4-year cell totals, a sealed 2026 NOAA-20 test, rules committed before the runs, and a claims-correction doc. They handle the Suomi NPP end, and add a recent-detections layer and weather context.
   - Weaker than us on cell size (2.5° vs ~1 km), start date (2003 vs Dec 2000) and creative UI.
2. **Aalvee-Aarham/kiln-watch**: 31 commits since 1 Nov.
   - 9 regions plus a worldwide 0.5° outlook globe.
   - Aqua drift measured, and a MODIS-denial test in 9 regions with its misses published (-9.7% to +27.5%).
   - 95% intervals, an early-warning backtest and an MCP server.
3. **sagoresarkerbdcse/nasa (FireCal v2)**
   - Claims 236 countries plus a 1° grid, a blind-test country-month error of 12-14%, and quantified Terra/Aqua drift.
   - Not deployed; real data is committed only for Bangladesh, India and Myanmar.
4. **SinuraWahalathanthri (EMBERLINE)**: a design twin of ours (hierarchical shrinkage plus LOYO, read in their code). One MVP commit, not deployed.
5. **Shahriar290900 (Parallax Fire)**: polished 3D globe with real satellite orbits and a story mode, but only 3 regions. A UX threat, not a science one.
6. **tttamannaanand/burn-calendar**: quantile mapping, 3 regions, live.

## Known repos since 1 Nov
- flare-chronos: polish only; the science is unchanged and it still covers two areas of interest.
- GeoHarmonix: a rebrand copy of flare-chronos.
- FireChronicle, EarthFire, firewatch-ai: dashboards without calibration.
- Ember-Line and samuelakosa/firecal: now 404.

## What rivals have that we lack
1. Global or any-area coverage (SHIFT strong; the others claims only).
2. A record running to the present, with the Suomi NPP end handled.
3. Quantified Terra/Aqua drift numbers.
4. Denial tests across many regions with misses published.
5. Extras: weather context, MCP, STAC/NetCDF, orbit globe.

## Where we still lead
- ~1 km cell-level shrinkage.
- Sealed exams on regions the model never saw, including a published failed round.
- 97.3% NOAA-20 handover agreement.
- The creative layer.

SHIFT's 4% is on 4-year cell totals, so it is not comparable with our 29.7% cell-month error.
